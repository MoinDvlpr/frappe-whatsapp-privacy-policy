This Privacy Policy explains how TX-MARKET (“we”, “us”, or “our”) handles information through our Frappe CRM service and its WhatsApp integration. The integration connects CRM records with the WhatsApp Business Platform provided by Meta so authorized business users can send and receive WhatsApp messages and manage related customer conversations.

1. Information we process

Depending on the features enabled and how your business uses the service, we may process the following categories of information:

  • Business and integration information: business name, business phone number, WhatsApp Business Account and phone-number identifiers, integration settings, and technical account identifiers.
  • Contact information: names, phone numbers, and other details a business user enters into CRM records.
  • Messages and attachments: message text, messages sent or received, files or media attached to a conversation, and related timestamps.
  • Message events: provider message identifiers and available sending, delivery, read, failure, or template-status information.
  • Support and security information: information included in support requests and technical logs required to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the service.

We do not ask users to include sensitive personal information in messages unless it is necessary for the business interaction and appropriate safeguards are in place.

2. How we use information

We use the information above as needed to:

  • connect Frappe CRM with the configured WhatsApp account;
  • send, receive, display, and associate messages with the relevant CRM contact, lead, deal, or other record;
  • show available message status and template information;
  • provide support, troubleshoot errors, maintain security, and prevent misuse; and
  • meet applicable legal obligations and enforce our service terms.

We will not use information for purposes incompatible with those described here without an appropriate legal basis or notice where required.

3. WhatsApp and other service providers

To provide WhatsApp messaging, the integration exchanges relevant information with Meta and WhatsApp through the WhatsApp Business Platform and related services. Their processing is also governed by their own terms and policies. Information may also be processed by the hosting, infrastructure, backup, and support providers used by the business that operates this service.

Access to CRM conversations should be limited to users authorized by the business. We do not sell personal information. Update this section if your deployment shares data with additional providers, analytics tools, AI services, or other third parties.

4. Business user responsibilities and message consent

The business using this integration is responsible for having an appropriate legal basis and any required permission to contact recipients, complying with applicable messaging and privacy rules, and honoring opt-out requests. Businesses must not use the service to send unlawful, deceptive, or unwanted messages. Recipients can use WhatsApp's available controls to block or report a business.

5. Data retention

Information is retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the CRM and messaging functions, maintain relevant business records, resolve disputes, meet legal obligations, and protect the service. Retention may vary by record type and by the business's configuration. Deleted information may remain temporarily in protected backups or logs until those copies are rotated or securely removed under the applicable retention process.
